The **Cooperative COA** is specifically designed for cooperative organizations with:\n\n- ✅ Member-specific accounts\n- ✅ Share capital tracking\n- ✅ Statutory reserves (required by law)\n- ✅ Patronage refund accounts\n- ✅ Member vs. non-member transaction tracking\n- ✅ Cooperative-specific income and expenses\n\n---\n\n## Structure & Account Categories\n\n### 1. **ASSETS (1000-2100)** - What the Cooperative Owns\n\n```\n1000 - Cash and Cash Equivalents\n  1010 - Cash on Hand\n  1020 - Cash in Banks\n  1030 - Petty Cash\n\n1100 - Member Receivables (loans/credit to members)\n1200 - Non-Member Receivables\n1300 - Merchandise Inventory\n1400 - Prepaid Expenses\n1600 - Short-term Investments\n1800 - Property, Plant & Equipment\n1900 - Accumulated Depreciation\n```\n\n### 2. **LIABILITIES (3000-3600)** - What the Cooperative Owes\n\n```\n3000 - Accounts Payable\n3100 - Accrued Expenses\n3200 - Short-term Loans Payable\n\n3300 - Member Deposits (COOPERATIVE-SPECIFIC)\n  3310 - Share Capital Subscription Payable\n  3320 - Member Savings Deposits Payable\n  3330 - Member Time Deposits Payable\n  3340 - Patronage Refunds Payable (UNIQUE TO COOPERATIVES)\n\n3500 - Long-term Loans Payable\n```\n\n### 3. **EQUITY (4000-4500)** - Member Ownership\n\n```\n4000 - Share Capital - Members\n4100 - Entrance Fees Paid In\n\n4200 - Statutory Reserves (REQUIRED BY LAW)\n  4210 - Legal Reserve Fund\n  4220 - Education and Training Reserve\n  4230 - Cooperative Development Fund\n  4240 - Emergency Reserve Fund\n  4250 - Community Service Reserve\n\n4400 - Retained Earnings\n4500 - Patronage Refund Reserve\n```\n\n### 4. **REVENUES (5000-5400)** - Income Sources\n\n```\n5000 - Revenue from Member Sales (TRACKS MEMBER VS NON-MEMBER)\n  5010 - Goods Sales to Members\n  5020 - Services Revenue from Members\n  5030 - Membership Fees Revenue\n\n5100 - Revenue from Non-Member Sales\n5200 - Interest Income\n5300 - Other Operating Income\n5400 - Grants and Donations\n```\n\n### 5. **EXPENSES** - Costs\n\n```\n6000 - Cost of Goods Sold\n\n7000 - Personnel Expenses\n7100 - Facility and Utilities\n7200 - Administrative Expenses\n7300 - Depreciation Expense\n7400 - Marketing and Promotion\n7500 - Member Education and Training (COOPERATIVE-SPECIFIC)\n7600 - Other Operating Expenses\n\n8000 - Financial Expenses (Interest)\n9000 - Income Tax Expense\n```\n\n---\n\n## Key Cooperative Features\n\n### Feature 1: Member vs. Non-Member Tracking\n\n**Why?** Cooperatives must distinguish between:\n- Revenue from member transactions\n- Revenue from non-member transactions\n- Patronage refunds go only to members\n\n**Accounts:**\n- `5010` - Goods Sales to Members\n- `5110` - Goods Sales to Non-Members\n\n**Example:**\n```\nMember buys $100 worth of goods:\n  Debit: Cash (1010)          $100\n  Credit: Member Sales (5010) $100\n\nNon-member buys $100:\n  Debit: Cash (1010)            $100\n  Credit: Non-Member Sales (5110) $100\n```\n\n### Feature 2: Member Deposits & Savings\n\n**Accounts:**\n- `3320` - Member Savings Deposits Payable (Savings Accounts)\n- `3330` - Member Time Deposits Payable (Fixed Deposits)\n- `3340` - Patronage Refunds Payable\n\n**Example:**\n```\nMember deposits $500 in savings:\n  Debit: Cash (1010)                        $500\n  Credit: Member Savings Deposits (3320)   $500\n```\n\n### Feature 3: Share Capital (Member Investment)\n\n**Accounts:**\n- `4010` - Common Share Capital\n- `4020` - Preferred Share Capital\n\n**Example:**\n```\nMember buys 10 shares at $100 each:\n  Debit: Cash (1010)              $1,000\n  Credit: Share Capital (4010)   $1,000\n```\n\n### Feature 4: Statutory Reserves\n\n**Required by cooperative law:** Must allocate portion of surplus to:\n\n1. **Legal Reserve** (account 4210)\n   - Typically 10% of annual surplus\n   - Used for emergencies/solvency\n\n2. **Education Reserve** (account 4220)\n   - Typically 5% of annual surplus\n   - For member training programs\n\n3. **Cooperative Development Fund** (account 4230)\n   - For cooperative growth\n\n**Example:**\n```\nIf cooperative has $10,000 surplus:\n  To Legal Reserve: $1,000 (10%)\n  To Education Fund: $500 (5%)\n  To Patronage: $3,000 (30%)\n  Retained: $5,500 (55%)\n```\n\n### Feature 5: Patronage Refunds\n\n**What?** Return portion of surplus to members based on their participation\n\n**Accounts:**\n- `4500` - Patronage Refund Reserve\n- `3340` - Patronage Refunds Payable\n\n**Example:**\n```\nMember participated in $5,000 of the cooperative's $50,000 revenue (10%)\n\nTotal surplus to distribute: $2,000\nMember's patronage refund: $200 (10% of $2,000)\n\nTransaction:\n  Debit: Patronage Refund Reserve (4500)      $200\n  Credit: Patronage Refunds Payable (3340)   $200\n```\n\n### Feature 6: Member Education & Training\n\n**Account:** `7500` - Member Education and Training\n\n**Examples:**\n- Cooperative orientation for new members\n- Skills training programs\n- Leadership development workshops\n\n---\n\n## How to Use in Your System\n\n### File: `coop_chart_of_accounts.py`\n\nContains the complete chart of accounts dictionary:\n\n```python\nfrom
